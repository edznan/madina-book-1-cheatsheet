
# MADINA BOOK 1 - GRAMMAR COURSE NOTES (Arabic + English)

---

## 1. Types of Words (أَقْسَامُ الكَلامِ)

**اِسْم** – noun  
*Used for people, objects, places. Roles: subject, object.*

**فِعْل** – verb  
*Denotes actions. Role: predicate.*

**حَرْف** – particle  
*Prepositions, conjunctions, connectors.*

---

## 2. Types of Verbs (أَنْوَاعُ الفِعْل)

**فِعْلٌ مَاضٍ** – past tense  
e.g. كتبَ – he wrote

**فِعْلٌ مُضَارِعٌ** – present tense  
e.g. يكتبُ – he writes

**فِعْلُ أَمْرٍ** – imperative  
e.g. اُكتبْ – write!

---

## 3. Demonstrative Pronouns (الإِشَارَة)

**هَذَا** – this (masculine)  
`هذا كتابٌ` – this is a book

**هَذِهِ** – this (feminine)  
`هذه سيارةٌ` – this is a car

**ذَلِكَ** – that (masculine)  
`ذلك بيتٌ` – that is a house

**تِلْكَ** – that (feminine)  
`تلك شجرةٌ` – that is a tree

---

## 4. Question Words (السُّؤَال)

**مَا هَذَا؟** – What is this?  
**مَنْ هَذَا؟** – Who is this?  
**أَيْنَ؟** – Where?  
**مَتَى؟** – When?  
**كَيْفَ؟** – How?

---

## 5. Shaddah (الشَّدَّة)

Shaddah ( ّ ) = doubled consonant.  
Example: مُدَرِّس = mudarris (with double R)

---

## 6. Singular and Plural (المُفْرَد وَالجَمْع)

**كتابٌ** – book (singular)  
**كُتُبٌ** – books (plural)

**طالبٌ** – student (singular)  
**طُلَّابٌ** – students (plural)

---

## 7. Moon and Sun Letters (حُرُوفُ القَمَر وَالشَّمْس)

The definite article **ال** is pronounced based on the first letter of the word:

**Sun Letters (shamsiyya):**  
L is merged and next letter is doubled.  
e.g. الشَّمْس → ash-shams

**Moon Letters (qamariyya):**  
L is pronounced clearly.  
e.g. القمر → al-qamar

---

## 8. Nominal Sentence (الجُمْلَة الاسْمِيَّة)

**مُبْتَدَأ** – subject (always nominative)  
**خَبَر** – predicate (also nominative)

Note: If mubtada’ is indefinite, context is needed (e.g., a question or negation).

---

## 9. Verbal Sentence (الجُمْلَة الفِعْلِيَّة)

**فِعْل** – verb  
**فَاعِل** – doer (subject)  
**مَفْعُول بِهِ** – object (if present)

Example:  
كتبَ الطالبُ الدرسَ – The student wrote the lesson.

---

## 10. Word Cases (الإِعْرَاب)

**مَرْفُوع** – nominative ( ُ ) → subject  
**مَنْصُوب** – accusative ( َ ) → object  
**مَجْرُور** – genitive ( ِ ) → after prepositions

---

## 11. Possessive Construction (الإِضَافَة)

**مُضَاف** – the possessed item (no AL, no tanwīn)  
**مُضَافٌ إِلَيْهِ** – the possessor (always genitive)

Example:  
بابُ المسجدِ – the door of the mosque

---

## 12. Prepositions (حُرُوفُ الجَرّ)

**في** – in  
**على** – on  
**من** – from  
**إلى** – to  
**مع** – with

Note: These always cause the following noun to be in genitive case (مَجْرُور).

---

## 13. Pronouns (ضَمَائِر)

**Attached Pronouns**  
كتابي – my book  
درسُهُ – his lesson

**Detached Pronouns**  
أنا – I  
نحن – we  
أنتَ – you (m)  
أنتِ – you (f)  
هو – he  
هي – she

---

## 14. Verb Conjugation with Pronouns

**أنا كتبتُ** – I wrote  
**نحن كتبنا** – We wrote  
**أنتَ كتبتَ** – You (m) wrote  
**أنتِ كتبتِ** – You (f) wrote  
**أنتم كتبتم** – You all (m) wrote  
**أنتن كتبتن** – You all (f) wrote  
**هو كتبَ** – He wrote  
**هي كتبتْ** – She wrote  
**هم كتبوا** – They (m) wrote  
**هن كتبن** – They (f) wrote

---

These are the foundational rules of Madina Book 1.
